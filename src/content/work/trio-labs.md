At Trio Labs, I worked on R&D equipment for precision metal additive manufacturing, including fixtures, printers, and post-processing hardware.

My work moved between CAD and the shop: designing parts, making them, assembling them, and finding out what needed to change. I used machining, sheet metal, and additive manufacturing, and helped bring equipment online.

Troubleshooting often crossed disciplines. A problem could come from the mechanism, an electrical connection, a sensor, or the process. I also worked with operator feedback on post-processing hardware and supported equipment validation through IQ/OQ/PQ activities.

The equipment designs and process details are confidential, so I’m keeping this overview brief.
